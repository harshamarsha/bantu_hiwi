Method: the pilot's evidence chain, run over the full table. Google Books (keyed) supplies edition dates and a `publicDomain`/full-view flag; Open Library supplies edition-level `publish_date`; Internet Archive and BnF corroborate; HathiTrust `rightsCode` is accepted only when its record dates match the edition. VIAF → Wikipedia → Wikidata → BnF life dates supply author deaths. Verdicts are the statutory US (95 years) and EU (life+70) terms, reconciled with that evidence.
